**Class 9 Science MCQs**

A curated collection of **Class 9 Science multiple-choice questions (MCQs)** designed 
to support effective learning, revision, and exam preparation. The repository covers 
important concepts across the Class 9 Science curriculum and provides answer keys for 
quick self-assessment.

**Key Features**

* Topic-wise Class 9 Science MCQs
* Clear and exam-oriented questions
* Answer keys for self-assessment
* Useful for students, revision, and practice
* Simple, organized, and beginner-friendly resources
